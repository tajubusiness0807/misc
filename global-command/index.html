    GLOBALCOMMAND

    Seven powers. Six continents. One world to hold.

    Choose your power Untick a power to leave it out of the war; its cities start neutral.

    How to play

    Goal

    Win by holding every starting capital, controlling 60% of all cities, or eliminating every rival. You lose if you lose all of your cities.

    Turns

    Each turn you collect credits from your cities (capitals give a bonus; holding every city in a region pays a region bonus). Every city can build one unit per turn. Units move, then attack. End your turn with E.

    Armies cost upkeep: you get 10 units plus one per city for free, and each unit beyond that costs 0.5 credits a turn. A freshly captured city is in unrest for 2 turns: no income and no production.

    Combat

    Damage depends on attack vs. defence, remaining HP and terrain. Defenders hit back if they can reach the attacker. Hover an enemy to see a forecast before you commit. Ranged units (artillery, battleships) are never counter-attacked.

    Terrain

    Forest +25%, hills +30%, mountains +60% defence; cities +50%, capitals +75%. Units that don't move entrench for an extra +20%. Land units must stop when they move next to an enemy land unit (zone of control).

    Crossing oceans

    Land units can embark by moving onto a coastal sea hex (this ends their move). At sea they move 4 hexes per turn but defend very poorly — escort them. Moving back onto land disembarks. Naval units may sail through your own port cities: hold Panama and Cairo to use the canals.

    Air power

    Fighters and bombers may keep moving after attacking. An aircraft that ends its turn away from a friendly city or carrier loses 3 HP. Anti-air units fire on aircraft attacking anything next to them.

    ICBMs

    Cities can build intercontinental missiles (30 credits, range 32). A strike deals 7 damage on the target hex and 3 to the hexes around it, and halves the target city's income for 3 turns. Enemy anti-air near the target may intercept it.

    Controls

    Click to select, click a highlighted hex to move or attack. Right-click or Esc cancels. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. N next unit, S skip unit, C go to capital, Space centre selection, arrows/WASD pan, +/- zoom.
